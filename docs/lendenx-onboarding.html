Getting around

Sign in like always. You'll land on a chooser screen with two tiles — pick one:

Orange

Lenden

The lending business. Loans, borrowers, interest — exactly what you're used to.

Blue

LendenX

The new cash‑in / cash‑out business. Its own balance, its own transactions, color-coded blue everywhere.

From inside either one, tap the small house icon in the top-left of the header to jump back to that chooser at any time. The Reports link next to it always shows your combined earnings across both businesses.

How a cash‑in works

Cash In
Customer's cash
→
You
→
Customer's GCash

Customer wants to load money onto their GCash. In the app:

  1. Customer hands you physical cash.
  2. You send the equivalent amount to their GCash.
  3. The small difference between what they gave you and what you sent is the fee — that's the profit, kept as physical cash on hand.

In the app: + New transaction → Cash In, then pick funding, then enter the amount. The fee is calculated for you.

How a cash‑out works

Cash Out
Customer's GCash
→
You
→
Customer's cash

Customer wants to withdraw money out of GCash. Same idea in reverse:

  1. Customer sends you GCash.
  2. You hand them the equivalent amount in physical cash.
  3. The difference is the fee, this time kept in the GCash balance.

In the app: + New transaction → Cash Out — same flow, same fee schedule either direction.

Pool vs. specific friends

Every cash‑in or cash‑out asks how it's funded:

From the shared pool

Drawn from LendenX's own balance. The fee splits equally, five ways, automatically.

Specific friends

One of us fronted our own cash or GCash for this one. We manually enter how the fee is split, since it's our own money in play.

The fee schedule

Same table, whether it's a cash‑in or a cash‑out — it's based on the amount only.

AmountFee
₱1 – ₱299₱10
₱300 – ₱599₱15
₱600 – ₱1,000₱20
Every extra ₱1,000 after that+₱20

Example — ₱1,299: ₱20 for the first ₱1,000, plus ₱10 for the ₱299 left over → ₱30 fee.

Odd amount, negotiated fee, whatever the exception is? There's a manual fee field for that. It's meant to be rare — the schedule above is the default for everything else.

Adding funds (not a transaction)

Add Funds

Need to top up LendenX's cash or GCash balance so there's float to work with? That's separate from logging a cash‑in/cash‑out. Use + Add funds — pick who's contributing, physical or digital, and the amount.

This only adds funds to the pool — no profit, no admin fee. It's capital, not a sale. Same warning shows in the app itself so it's never confused with a real transaction.

Moving money between the two

Lenden's lending pool and LendenX's balance are kept separate on purpose — but money can move either direction when needed, logged from the LendenX Pool page: lending pool → LendenX physical or digital, or back the other way. Both sides' balances update to match.

Checking earnings

Three places to look, depending on how wide a view you want:

  • Reports Combined — both businesses, per friend, in one table. This is the header link, reachable from anywhere.
  • Lenden → Reports Lending only — same detailed report you had before.
  • LendenX → Reports Exchange only — includes Katch's and Tita A's running totals.
